The Challenge: Manual & Fragmented Planning

Siloed phone/memo coordination between departments

  • •
    Manual, siloed block planning: Engineering (P-Way), Electrical (OHE), and Signal (S&T) apply independently on spreadsheets with zero integrated visibility.
  • •
    Train–block conflicts discovered too late: Blocks frequently cancelled at the last hour or forced to truncate when Rajdhani/Vande Bharat trains approach.
  • •
    No integration of asset health and train schedules: Urgent maintenance is repeatedly deferred, leading to speed restrictions and track safety concerns.
  • •
    No dynamic re-planning when trains are delayed: When upstream trains run 30+ minutes late, pre-sanctioned blocks become invalid with no instant alternative slot.

The Solution: RAILSETU AI Synthesis

Mathematical constraint optimization with human sign-off

  • •
    Automatic feasible block windows: CP-SAT mathematical optimization scans corridor traffic schedules in seconds to propose minimal-disruption slots.
  • •
    Clear impact on trains, delays & resources: Real-time estimation of passenger delay minutes, priority protection, and gang machine availability.

Operational Protocol

How It Works in 6 Steps

Safety constraints are hard rules; AI recommends, humans decide.

01 Intake

Maintenance Request Submission

Field section engineers submit maintenance demands specifying corridor section, work nature (track renewal, OHE, signal), duration, required machines, and acceptable date windows.

02 Validation

Data Validation & Conflict Pre-Check

RAILSETU aggregates scheduled timetables, simulated train positions, and upstream blocks across neighboring divisions to establish corridor constraints.

03 Safety Rules

Feasible Window Detection

Constraint models identify natural traffic lull intervals. Safety rules enforce non-negotiable 15-minute headway buffers before and after blocks.

04 Optimization

Optimization & Ranking of Plans

Candidates are evaluated on a multi-objective function: minimize passenger delay minutes, strictly protect Rajdhani/Vande Bharat paths, and optimize machine utilization.

05 Simulation

What-If Simulation & Comparison

Controllers can test sudden train delays or emergency rail fractures, dragging the block on an interactive Gantt chart to instantly visualize collision warnings and delay metrics.

06 Execution

Approval, Execution & Dynamic Re-Plan

The Chief Controller grants digital authorization. If upstream trains suffer en-route delays, RAILSETU dynamically calculates re-planned slots to prevent traffic gridlock.
